FAQ
How are DRAW, YOUR and LIFE different?
Each course includes everything in the one before it. DRAW covers 12-season draping, color makeup and lip color recommendations, plus your results sheet and color card. YOUR adds fashion styling and jewelry, hair, brow and nail design, contact lens and eyeglasses design, a fragrance recommendation and a makeup pouch check. LIFE is the total course, adding AI analysis of facial and body bone structure with makeup and fashion styling coaching.
How long does it take and how much is it?
For one person: DRAW 40 min ₩110,000, YOUR 80 min ₩200,000, LIFE 110 min ₩300,000. The more people, the lower the price per person — DRAW for 3 is ₩85,000 each. For groups of 4 or more, please contact us. See the price table above for details.
How does it work if we book together?
You all go through the session together at the same time, and the per-person price drops as the group grows. DRAW, YOUR and LIFE can each be booked for up to 3 people; for 4 or more, please contact us.
What do I take home?
You receive our own results sheet and a color card to carry while shopping. YOUR adds a tone-specific guide, and LIFE adds a total image-making guide.

Makeup Class + Facial Bone Structure Diagnosis

Most of us have copied a look from YouTube or social media exactly, only to find it looked off on our own face. Bone structure, skin tone and facial asymmetry differ from person to person — so instead of the same makeup as everyone else, there is a way of doing makeup that is right for you.

CURRICULUM

Hands-on makeup lesson curriculum

From basic skincare to color makeup, you learn 1:1, in detail, how to truly understand your face and cover what bothers you.

Pre-consultation & image consulting
We look at the image you want and your everyday makeup concerns, and set the direction of your personal lesson.
Common concerns
1
Skincare & base that lasts
The right skincare routine, which decides how well your makeup turns out, and techniques for a thin base that really adheres.
2
Contouring & brows for your face shape
Add dimension with contour and highlighter that match your bone structure, and learn to shape and naturally draw your brows — the feature that defines your impression most — to suit your face shape.
3
Eye makeup that refines your eye shape
Up close, we show you where to place shadow for your eye shape — single or double lids, the shape of the outer corner — how to draw eyeliner, and how to use mascara that won’t smudge.
4

Find the blush placement that gives your face natural color and dimension, and learn lip makeup that suits the shape of your lips.

FAQ
Can I wear makeup to the diagnosis?
For an accurate diagnosis, please come without makeup. Please skip tone-up sunscreen that covers your skin tone as well. If you remove makeup at the studio, that time counts toward your session, so coming bare-faced lets you use the full time for the diagnosis.
Who will do my diagnosis?
Our director, who holds a PhD in beauty arts (makeup) and an MA in hair and is a university professor of beauty. She has written many academic papers on personal color and cosmetics, including KCI-indexed papers, and has done more than 20,000 diagnoses since 2016.
